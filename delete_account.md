# Account Deletion Policy

**Last Updated:** August 2, 2026

At **Freelance Finance**, we respect your privacy and provide you with the ability to permanently delete your account and associated personal data.

## How to Delete Your Account

You can request deletion of your account using one of the following methods:

* **Within the App:** Go to **Settings → Account → Delete Account** and follow the on-screen instructions.

## What Data Is Deleted

When your account is deleted, we permanently remove the following information:

* Account information (such as your email address and authentication details)
* Income records
* Expense records
* Invoice records
* Financial goals and budgets
* Any other personal financial data associated with your account

## Data Retention

Unless required by applicable law, we do not retain your personal financial data after your account has been deleted.

If certain information must be retained to comply with legal, regulatory, fraud prevention, or security obligations, it will be retained only for the period required by law and will not be used for any other purpose.

## Processing Time

Account deletion requests are typically processed immediately or within **30 days** of receiving the request.

If you have any questions about account deletion or your personal data, please contact us at:
